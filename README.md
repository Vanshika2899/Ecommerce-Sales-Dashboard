# Ecommerce-Sales-Dashboard-

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Ecommerce Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, order trends, and channel-wise contributions. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Data Visualization techniques.

The objective of this project is to help businesses identify key revenue drivers, understand customer demographics, and make data-driven decisions to improve sales performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

An ecommerce business wants to understand:

- Sales and order trends throughout the year
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Top-performing states
- Best sales channels
- Highest contributing customer segments
- Product category performance

Using Excel analytics, the dashboard answers these questions and provides strategic recommendations.

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions Solved

1. Compare Sales and Orders using a single chart.
2. Which month recorded the highest Sales and Orders?
3. Who purchased more in 2022 – Men or Women?
4. What were the different Order Status categories?
5. Which are the Top 10 States contributing to sales?
6. What is the relationship between Age Group and Gender based on orders?
7. Which Sales Channel contributes the maximum revenue?
8. Which Product Category is the highest-selling?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Customer Insights

- Women contribute approximately **65% of total purchases**.
- The **Adult Age Group (30–49 years)** contributes nearly **50% of total sales**.
- Female customers represent the most valuable customer segment.

### Geographic Insights

Top contributing states:

1. Maharashtra
2. Karnataka
3. Uttar Pradesh

These states generate the highest share of overall sales.

### Channel Performance

The highest sales contributions come from:

- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Myntra

These platforms drive the majority of customer purchases.
